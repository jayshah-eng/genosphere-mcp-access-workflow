Helix GenoSphere™ MCP Connector

Inbound Access Request — Intake, Triage & Provisioning Workflow
Three intake channels → triage → routing → demo → provisioning · Launch phase, June 2026
① Intake — three channels
Channel A · Website
Form submission
Requester completes the website HubSpot form (linked from the connector listing + resource page). Self-selects segment.
Channel B · Email
Email inquiry
Partner emails the centralized alias mcp@helix.com — preferred for institutional partners who shouldn't have to fill a form.
Channel C · Other sources
Any other inbound
Other Helix websites/landing pages, direct emails to individual reps, referrals, event/booth leads, or Anthropic-directory clicks. All are funneled to mcp@helix.com to enter this workflow.
Channel C is a catch-all: any inbound that isn't the website form is redirected to mcp@helix.com, so it merges into the email path for triage.
② Triage — flag & route
Form path
Marketing triages
Marketing reviews the submission and routes it to the Health Systems or Life Sciences team based on segment + email domain.
Owner: Marketing (Jay Shah & Charlotte Franco)
Email path
Inbox team flags
All non-form inbound (Channels B & C) lands in the closed team inbox (mcp@helix.com). Inbox members flag each request and assign it to HS or LS.
Owner: mcp@helix.com inbox team
③ Routing — segment owner works with the requester
LIFE SCIENCES / BIOTECH
LS team
Qualify & set up
LS BD works with the individual. Existing partners fast-tracked; new prospects → demo, slides, NDA. Confirm Anthropic enterprise agreement (ZDR) + MCP T&Cs accepted.
Owner: Life Sciences BD team
HEALTH SYSTEMS / HRN
HS team
Qualify & set up
HS BD / site DA works with the individual. Existing HRN site → its DA; new prospect → demo. Confirm Anthropic enterprise agreement (ZDR) + MCP T&Cs accepted.
Owner: Health Systems BD / site DA
④ Demo meeting — present, then align on access
Live session with the requester
MCP overview, access walkthrough & brief demo
The demo meeting is run by Basil Khuder / Product, with BD and DA support, and covers: (1) the MCP overview slides — what GenoSphere is, the value, and the aggregate/read-only guardrails; (2) how to access it — enterprise/ZDR + T&Cs prerequisites, the MCP group/role, and Okta sign-in; and (3) a brief live demo of the connector answering a sample question in Claude.
Owner: Basil Khuder / Product (with BD & DA support)
⑤ Provisioning — handoff from BD to access owners
Why a handoff exists
BD and/or DAs confirm & route; access owners complete provisioning
Before the handoff, BD and/or DAs confirm the partner is eligible, the Anthropic enterprise agreement (zero-data retention) is in place, and MCP T&Cs are accepted. The role-add is then completed by the access owners, with BD and/or DAs staying involved through activation. The provisioning model differs by segment — Life Sciences is centralized; Health Systems is site-controlled.
Gate before handoff: eligible region · enterprise/ZDR confirmed · T&Cs accepted
LIFE SCIENCES — centralized provisioning
Step 5A·1
LS team files a DEX ticket
Once approved, the LS team submits a ticket to the DEX team requesting the MCP permission for the named user. LS does not add the role itself.
Owner: Life Sciences BD → DEX
Step 5A·2
DEX adds the MCP group
DEX adds the dedicated group (anthropic-enterprise-mcp) to that user. Note: many LS customers are not SSO-integrated, so Helix — not the customer's IT — controls the mapping. This is the spoofing/personal-account risk the team flagged.
Owner: DEX team
HEALTH SYSTEMS / HRN — site-controlled
Step 5B·1
Helix qualifies the institution
The site engages its DA to request access for the institution. Helix confirms the site qualifies (enterprise agreement + ZDR), then enables the site to manage the MCP group itself.
Owner: site DA + Helix
Step 5B·2
Site adds the group to its users
The site adds the special MCP group to each named user — controlled by the site, exactly like Explorer & Genesis access today. Must be done explicitly per person, including users who already have other access.
Owner: site IdP / admin (self-service)
Step 6 · both paths converge — technical authorization
User connects in Claude → system verifies before any data
User enables Helix GenoSphere in Claude → authentication popup → sign in via Okta → the connector checks authorization on connect and on every tool call: valid MCP group, valid enterprise agreement, and current T&Cs version accepted. Only then is the connection enabled and queries run. Access is restricted to Claude (no general MCP clients) to shrink the attack surface.
Owner: Engineering · enforced automatically · access is read-only, aggregate-only

Key rules carried through the workflow

  • LSHS Every user needs a new MCP-specific group/role added by their IdP — no one gets access on Day 1, even existing Explorer users.
  • All serviceable partners must hold an Anthropic enterprise agreement with zero-data retention, accessed via a corporate (not personal) Claude account.
  • BD teams route; they don't provision. Life Sciences = centralized (BD files a DEX ticket; DEX adds the role). Health Systems = site-controlled (the site adds the role itself, like Explorer/Genesis).
  • The MCP group must be added explicitly per named user — including people who already hold Explorer/Genesis access.
  • Authorization is enforced by the connector on connect and on every tool call (valid group + enterprise/ZDR + current T&Cs); access is restricted to Claude only.
  • Out-of-region (outside North America & Europe), academic, and general-public requests are not serviceable at launch — logged to the interested-parties list.
