Key rules & legal notes

  • ZDR is the gate. Confirm an Anthropic ZDR agreement (non-negotiable) before scheduling any demo — don't waste a demo on someone who can't use the tool.
  • NDA differs by segment. LS new prospects sign an institutional NDA (lead-gen); HS partners are already covered by collaboration/confidentiality agreements — no new NDA.
  • Two consent levels. Institution signs the ZDR side letter; each individual accepts platform T&Cs at account creation (checkbox, no DocuSign).
  • Owners. The commercial owner (DA for HS, LS BD for LS) drives legal prereqs — Basil is technical/demo only.
  • Open items. Confirm marketplace-initiated access still enforces T&Cs (Nicole/Matt); clarify SSO for MCP (Jim); unified T&Cs still in development (Legal).
Health Systems / HRN — detailed access workflow

For existing HRN partners, the MCP is treated as a feature of the research platform, so access builds on the same Explorer SSO the site already controls. Steps below follow Partner Success onboarding (per Cindy).

1
Partner initiates interest
Via the Commercial team, Partner Success, or the webform. Any channel is fine — all roads lead to intake.
Partner → Commercial / Partner Success / webform
2
Log to the intake tracker
Partner Success adds the partner to the intake tracker.
Partner Success
3
DA responds with intro + requirements
DA shares intro information and the access requirements: (a) has Explorer SSO, (b) is a Claude enterprise customer with zero data retention, (c) will sign Helix's ZDR agreement.
DA (Delivery/Account lead)
4
Demo — at DA discretion
If no concrete use case yet, defer the demo until requirements are confirmed. If there's strong interest, demo earlier. JOCs are a natural venue for a Partner Success demo-lite; Basil Khuder supports larger, technical groups.
DA · Basil / Product for technical demos
5
Confirm requirements, then DA submits a SUPP ticket
Once the three requirements are confirmed, the DA files a SUPP ticket including: partner name; confirmation of Explorer SSO access (just the SSO — users don't need to be in the Explorer group yet); and the action “Need access to MCP Connector.” If the partner lacks Explorer SSO, they complete the Explorer steps first.
DA → SUPP ticket
6
DEX adds the partner to the approved MCP list
The DEX team adds the HRN partner to the approved list for MCP access.
DEX team
7
Partner IT adds users to the MCP group
The partner's IT administrator adds the desired users to the “MCP” user group through their Explorer SSO — the site controls this, just like Explorer/Genesis today.
Partner IT administrator
8
Partner IT distributes access via Claude enterprise
Partner IT updates the user Claude configuration and distributes MCP access through each user's Claude enterprise account.
Partner IT administrator
9
User authenticates & activates
The user follows the GenoSphere MCP Getting Started Guide: enable the connector in Claude → Okta sign-in → accept platform T&Cs. Access is read-only, aggregate-only, Claude-only.
Authorized user

Access requirements (all three must be met)

  • Health System has Explorer SSO access (if not, that's step #1 — go through the Explorer guide first).
  • They are a Claude enterprise customer with zero data retention.
  • They sign Helix's ZDR agreement (the GenoSphere MCP Access Agreement).
